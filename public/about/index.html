about

this is a personal research blog of andy (l), tracking my engagements with the digital and the affective. i’m interested in questions of interaction and negotiation with varieties of medium and material most notable of our contemporary digitized landscape, but certainly not only. i also run in circuits of rhythm and music, albeit in a oblique way.

i’ll add more as i go along
